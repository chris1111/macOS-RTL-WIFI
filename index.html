Wi-Fi for Realtek USB dongles on Intel macOS

A kernel extension and a menu bar app for OpenCore and Clover Hackintoshes. Choose your network once and it rejoins by itself after a reboot, usually in 1 to 3 seconds.

Download the latest release View the source

13 adapters tested on macOS Ventura and Tahoe. Free and open source under GPL-2.0.

Every network in range

The menu lists each network with its security type, channel and signal strength, strongest first. Choose one, enter the router password, and the app remembers it.

  • Both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks show up, on adapters that support 5 GHz.
  • When a join fails, the app explains the likely cause, such as a wrong password.
  • Rescan, rejoin or forget a network from the same menu.

Install in three steps

The installer package puts the kext in your EFI folder, adds the menu bar app and its launch agent, and edits the OpenCore config.plist for you.

  1. Run the installer

    Download Release.zip, open the package and choose the OpenCore or Clover driver. An uninstaller is included.

  2. Restart

    The kext loads at boot from your EFI partition, so a restart is required once.

  3. Pick your network

    Plug in the dongle, click the RTLWiFiBar icon, choose a network and enter the router password.

The adapter shows up as an Ethernet-style interface ("802.11n NIC") in System Settings, Network, not in the macOS Wi-Fi menu. Use the RTLWiFiBar menu to choose networks.

What to expect

Known limits

  • After a Wi-Fi drop in the middle of a session, the connection does not return by itself. A reboot rejoins normally.
  • A replacement dongle appears as a new network service ("NIC 2") because macOS identifies interfaces by MAC address.
  • Station mode with WPA2-Personal only. Intel Macs only.
